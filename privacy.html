Privacy Policy

Updated 7 October 2026 · TransMove Zimbabwe

TransMove connects passengers, transport customers, drivers and machinery owners in Zimbabwe. This policy covers our website, Android app and iPhone web app, including transport requests, driver offers, machinery bookings and account verification.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number, account role, profile photo, service area and optional biography.
  • Service information: pickup and destination addresses, location coordinates when you allow location access, trip dates, fares, offers, booking status, messages, ratings and reviews.
  • Provider information: vehicle and machinery descriptions, photos, availability, prices, driver's licenses, identity or registration documents and insurance documents submitted for verification.
  • Payment information: payment references, receipt images, subscription records and manual payment verification records you submit.
  • Technical information: authentication/session records and operational logs used to run the service and investigate failures. Your device manages permission choices for location, notifications and photos.

How we use information

We use information to maintain accounts, match requests with providers, display offers and approved listings, manage bookings, communicate about jobs, verify providers and payments, respond to support requests and protect the service from misuse. Profile photos identify your account. Verification documents help administrators review eligibility to provide services.

Google Drive access and Google user data

TransMove uses Google Drive as file storage for uploaded profile photos, provider verification documents, vehicle and machinery photos, and payment receipts. The storage account owner authorizes the connection; customers do not need to connect their own Google Drive accounts to upload files.

The configured Google Drive permission allows the server to find or create TransMove folders, upload files, read metadata, retrieve files for authorized viewing and manage associated stored files. This permission is broader than access to an individual file. TransMove uses it for the files and folders involved in its storage workflow, rather than to provide advertising or analyze unrelated personal Drive content.

The Google authorization credentials stay on the trusted server. They are not included in the mobile apps or sent to ordinary users. Driver verification documents and payment receipts are delivered through server access checks. Profile photos and listing photos can be shown to other users where needed to identify accounts and browse the marketplace.

TransMove's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, used for targeted advertising or used to train general-purpose AI models.

Who receives information

Relevant request, offer, route, contact and booking information is shared with participants so they can arrange their service. Reviews and approved listing details are visible in the relevant marketplace views. TransMove administrators access information needed for verification, support and service administration.

Service providers process information needed to operate TransMove: Supabase for database and account services, Google Drive for file storage and Render for website and server hosting. Map services receive information needed to display maps or calculate routes when you use those features. Providers may process information outside Zimbabwe. We may disclose information where legally required or to investigate fraud and protect users.

Retention and deletion

Account information and service records are kept while needed to operate your account, fulfil bookings, resolve disputes, maintain payment or verification records and meet applicable obligations. Different records can be needed for different periods; this policy does not promise a fixed deletion deadline.

You can request access, correction or deletion of account information and uploaded files using the contact details below. Identify your account and the information concerned; do not send passwords or authorization tokens. We may verify your identity and retain records required for unresolved transactions, security investigations or legal obligations. Residual copies may remain in provider backups according to their retention processes.

Your choices and security

You can update your profile and manage device permissions. Declining location access may require entering addresses manually. The Google storage account owner can revoke TransMove's access in their Google account; doing so prevents related uploads and file retrieval until the connection is restored.

We use authenticated accounts, server-side access checks and private document retrieval to restrict sensitive information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account credentials private and report suspected unauthorized access.
