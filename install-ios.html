TransMove on your iPhone

Install the TransMove web app on your home screen. It uses the live website and receives website updates when you reopen it online.

  1. Open the TransMove website in Safari.
  2. Tap Share in Safari. Depending on your version, it may be inside the menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. If shown, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.

This installs the web app. The native App Store version will be linked here after it is published.
